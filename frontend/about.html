Our Mission

To make healthcare accessible for everyone in Bhagalpur by eliminating long waiting times at clinics. We believe no one should spend hours waiting when they could be at home, at work, or with family.

📅

1. Book Online

Choose your clinic, doctor, and preferred time slot — all in 30 seconds.

📍

2. Track Live

Know exactly when your turn comes with real-time queue tracking.

🏥

3. Visit on Time

Arrive when it's your turn. Save 2+ hours per clinic visit.

👤

For Patients

Skip the queue. Book from home. Track your position live. No more endless waiting at clinics.

👨‍⚕️

💬 A Message from the Founder

"Growing up in Bhagalpur, I saw my parents and neighbors spend hours waiting at clinics — not knowing when their turn would come. The frustration of waiting endlessly while feeling unwell is something every Bhagalpuri understands. Apna Bhagalpur is my way of solving that problem for our city. I built this platform so that no one in Bhagalpur has to waste their precious time standing in queues. This is for my people, my city — Bhagalpur."
